    The rules this covers

    RuleStatusWhat it asks for
    EU CBAMDefinitive period since 1 January 2026Importers surrender certificates for 2026 imports in 2027. The CBAM factor rises from 2.5% in 2026 to 100% in 2034. An annual 50-tonne threshold applies, and aluminium counts direct emissions.
    UK CBAMStarts 1 January 2027Covers aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen, and iron and steel. Direct emissions only until at least 2029. HM Treasury sets a quarterly sector rate from the UK ETS price. Registration at £50,000 of goods in 12 months, and one default value per good.
    UAE climate lawIn force since 30 May 2025Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2024 requires entities that emit to measure, report and reduce emissions. Full compliance was due by 30 May 2026 unless extended, with reported fines of AED 50,000 to 2 million.

    Check the official texts before relying on any figure, starting with the UK government policy paper.

    How it works: each facility model converts its parameters into t CO₂e per tonne of product (smelter: cell energy, anode carbon, PFC slope method and alumina supply; refinery: fuel, power, bauxite and chemicals; recycling: melting fuel, power, scrap preparation and primary metal added). EU CBAM: certificates = embedded emissions minus benchmark × (1 − CBAM factor), priced at the certificate price. UK CBAM: direct emissions × the sector rate you enter. Parameter values are illustrative and must be replaced with measured, verified data. EGA is named only to cite its public figures, and this product is not affiliated with or endorsed by EGA.
